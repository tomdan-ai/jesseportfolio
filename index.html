Studio

Nine years of making software feel drawn by hand. I started in 2D cel animation, which is why my interfaces still obey squash, stretch and follow-through.

I work directly with product and engineering, usually from the second sprint onward. A typical engagement: storyboards in week one, a motion sheet in week two, then production files — .riv, .json, or an SVG sprite sheet — wired into your build before the handoff meeting ends.

I keep one rule from the animation desk: if a transition needs a label to be understood, it's the wrong transition.
